About

Academic Profile

I am an Assistant Professor (Sr. Gr.) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amritapuri. My academic and research interests focus on machine learning, thermal imaging, data mining, and algorithms, with applications in intelligent image understanding.

My research includes thermal image synthesis and refinement, data augmentation under data scarcity, mixed-pixel identification and correction, and thermally aware generative modelling.
